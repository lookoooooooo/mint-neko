About Sasakia Charonda 🐛
This creature is famous for its silly, cat-like face mask and tiny dragon horns. It uses these horns to blend in perfectly with the leaves of Chinese Hackberry trees. It strictly feeds on minty greens and changes its color to a chocolate brown shade to hibernate during cold winters!
